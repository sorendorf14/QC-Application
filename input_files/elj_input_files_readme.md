# Readme detailing all column information for Elijah Tall Tower input files without included column names. 

## Column explanations for Elijah Tall Tower text files (e.g., ElijahTallTower.20251123.txt)

Columns:
1. UTC computer time
2. UTC logger time
3. Panel temperature (C)
4. Battery voltage (V)
5. Air temperature (C) level 1 (top), 30 m
6. Air temperature (C) level 2, 15 m
7. Air temperature (C) level 3, 7.5 m
8. Air temperature (C) level 4, 4 m
9. Air temperature (C) level 5, 2 m
10. Air temperature (C) level 6 (lowest), 1 m
11. Pressure (hPa) -600
12. Wind speed (m/s) level 1, 30 m
13. Wind speed (m/s) level 2, 15 m
14. Wind speed (m/s) level 3, 7.5 m
15. Wind speed (m/s) level 4, 4 m
16. Wind speed (m/s) level 5, 2 m
17. Wind speed (m/s) level 6, 1m
18. Wind direction (degrees) level 1, 30 m
19. Wind direction (degrees) level 2, 15 m
20. Wind direction (degrees) level 3, 7.5 m
21. Wind direction (degrees) level 4, 4 m
22. Wind direction (degrees) level 5, 2 m
23. Wind direction (degrees) level 6, 1 m
24. Relative humidity temperature (C) level 1, 30 m
25. Relative humidity (%) level 1, 30 m
26. Relative humidity temperature (C) level 2, 15 m
27. Relative humdiity (%) level 2, 15 m
28. Relative humidity temperature (C) level 3, 7.5 m
29. Relative humidity (%) level  3, 7.5 m
30. Relative humidity temperature (C) level 4, 4 m
31. Relative humidity (%) level 4, 4 m
32. Acoustic Distance Gauge raw distance (m)
33. ADG Quality number
34. ADG Calculated Distance (m)
35. Pressure microseconds (from Paroscientific pressure gauge)
36. Temperature microseconds (from Paroscientific pressure gauge)
37. Average Detector Temperature (C) (from skin temperature sensor)
38. Average Skin temperature (C)
39. Average Shortwave up (W/m^2)
40. Average shortwave down (W/m^2)
41. Average longwave up raw (W/m^2)
42. Average longwave down raw (W/m^2)
43. Average NR01 temperature (C)
44. Average NR01 temperature (Kelvin)
45. Average corrected longwave up  (W/m^2) (correcting for sensor body temperature)
46. Average corrected longwave down (W/m^2)  (correcting for sensor body temperature)
